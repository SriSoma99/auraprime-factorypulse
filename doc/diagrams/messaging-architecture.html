AuraPrime FactoryPulse Messaging Architecture

How a reading travels from the plant to an incident: Mosquitto at each site, the Ingest Gateway as the only bridge, Kafka as the durable backbone, and consumer groups that commit only after processing. When anything slows down, the pressure moves back towards the edge, where telemetry is buffered. Alerts are never dropped.
