Amendment 2026-11-06 (ADR-041): adding, reviewing and removing documents

  • Add a document. Pick a type (equipment manual, standard operating procedure, troubleshooting guide) and the equipment from the plant registry; enter title, version and effective date; "Insert template" fills the required headings. The local build also offers sample documents for the signed-in tenant's equipment.
  • Refused upload. "Check and upload" lists every problem under its field (for example "Title needs at least 8 characters", "Add the section ## Safety"). Nothing is stored.
  • Pending review. An accepted upload is listed as Pending review and is not used in answers. Approve is disabled for the uploader ("another person must approve it").
  • Delete or withdraw. Each document has Delete (never cited) or Withdraw (cited by an answer, incident or work order), which opens a reason field; confirm stays disabled until the reason is long enough. Withdrawn documents stay listed with their reason.
  • Filters: All, Pending review, Approved, Withdrawn, each with a count. States: loading, error, empty, ready; view only disables Add, Approve and Delete. Every change shows its audit reference.
  • RAG-1: Technical documents ingested with section anchors for 100% cited diagnoses.
  • RAG-5 (Version superseding): When v4 of the Weld Transformer Manual is approved, v3 chunks are automatically marked SUPERSEDED and excluded from active incident diagnosis.
  • RAG-10 (Quarantine gate): External or unverified PDF uploads cannot be indexed by role agents until approved by an ACT-4 Reliability Engineer.
