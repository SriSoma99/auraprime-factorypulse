W14 Plant equipment (first screen)

Every line, station, machine and sensor of the site with its configured limits and live health, before alerts or approvals. Health and machine state are shown by symbol and label, never by colour alone (UI-6).

Symbols carry the state: ● OK · ▲ out of range · ▬ flatline · ◌ stale · ○ no data. Limits come from the equipment registry, not from documents (RAG-11).

Implements 02 section 6.1 row W14 and ADR-039. Requirements: ING-7, ING-12, UI-4, UI-6. Data: GET /v1/plant, polled at app.poll_interval_ms.

  • Health precedence: no data, stale (silent longer than the sensor's max silence), out of range, flatline, OK. A flatline only counts while the machine runs or its state is unknown.
  • Machine state comes from the machine's state sensor and its run and stop thresholds; without a fresh reading it is "state unknown", never assumed running.
  • The filter hides OK sensors only; stations and machines stay, so the line stays recognisable. "No data" counts as needing attention.
  • The phone layout stacks stations in line order; each station card takes the full width.
