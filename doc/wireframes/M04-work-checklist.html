Implements 02 section 6.2 row M04, the mobile rules in section 12 and the verification design in 03 section 12. Requirements: UI-5, LRN-1 (Community), LRN-2 (Full); acceptance criteria UI-AC-1, UI-AC-10.

  • Safety reminders are quoted only from approved, versioned procedures and always show their citation. At L3 retrieval is down, so the reminder is shown from the last known good version and flagged unverified (03 section 13.2).
  • Readings are checked against the instrument range and the reference range; reference values are synthetic and come from configuration and the asset registry, not the design.
  • Offline: steps, readings and photos queue with idempotency keys; notes merge on sync, approvals follow server-wins (02 section 12).
  • Wording of safety text is pending domain review (UO-4).
