# Low clamp pressure on the Acme press: troubleshooting guide

Synthetic sample document for the Acme Test Plant tenant (ADR-019). Not a real site procedure.

## Symptom

Pressure sensor PT-X1 on the Acme press hydraulic unit reads below its normal range during press cycles, or the Plant screen shows PT-X1 as out of range low. Parts may come out of the press under-formed.

## Possible causes

The most likely causes, in order, are a blocked return-line filter, a low oil level, a relief valve that is set too low or sticking open, a worn pump, and a faulty or drifting pressure sensor.

## Checks

First look at the Plant screen: if PT-X1 shows stale or flatline, suspect the sensor or its connection rather than the hydraulics. Check the filter clogging indicator and the oil level in the sight glass. Listen for a whining pump, which points to a low oil level or air in the oil. Compare PT-X1 with the mechanical gauge on the manifold; if the gauge reads normal, the sensor is drifting.

## Corrective action

Change the filter element using the return-line filter procedure. Top up the oil to the mark on the sight glass. If the relief valve is suspect, ask the reliability engineer to check its setting. Raise a work order for a sensor calibration if the gauge and PT-X1 disagree. Replace the pump only after the other causes are ruled out.

## Safety

Never adjust the relief valve while the press is cycling. Apply lockout and release stored pressure before opening any part of the circuit, and keep hands clear of the press ram during test cycles.
