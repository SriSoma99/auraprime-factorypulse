# Return-line filter change on the Acme press hydraulic unit

Synthetic sample document for the Acme Test Plant tenant (ADR-019). Not a real site procedure.

## Purpose

This procedure replaces the return-line filter element on the Acme press hydraulic unit so that contamination does not wear the pump or make the clamp pressure unstable.

## Scope

It applies to asset as-x-press at the Acme press station on Acme line 1. It is carried out by a trained maintenance technician and checked by the shift lead.

## Safety

Stop the press at the end of a cycle. Apply personal lockout to the motor isolator and try the start button to prove the motor will not run. Open the dump valve and wait until pressure sensor PT-X1 reads zero before touching any fitting. Wear safety glasses and oil-resistant gloves.

## Procedure

1. Place a drip tray under the filter housing.
2. Unscrew the filter bowl slowly so any trapped pressure can escape.
3. Remove the old element and check it for metal particles; keep it if you find any.
4. Wipe the bowl clean, fit a new element of the part number on the housing label and replace the bowl seal.
5. Tighten the bowl by hand, then a further quarter turn.
6. Close the dump valve, remove your lock and start the motor.

## Verification

Run the press for five cycles and watch for leaks at the filter housing. Confirm on the Plant screen that PT-X1 is back inside its normal range and the clogging indicator is green. Record the change and any metal particles in the work order.
