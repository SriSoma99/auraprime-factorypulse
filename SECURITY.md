# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Do not open a public issue.

Include the affected component, steps to reproduce and the impact you observed. You will get an acknowledgement, and a fix or mitigation plan once the report is confirmed.

## Scope

In scope: the code in this repository, including the safety invariants listed in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). A bypass of tenant isolation, of human-only approval or of the audit chain is treated as critical.

Out of scope: the synthetic demo data, and deployments you operate yourself.

AuraPrime FactoryPulse is advisory software and must not be connected to equipment control or safety functions.
